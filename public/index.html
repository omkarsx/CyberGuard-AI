Threat Landscape 2026

Why Cybersecurity Matters Now More Than Ever

As India’s digital economy processes billions of transactions monthly, deceptive social engineering attacks have grown exponentially in speed and sophistication.

Online Financial Fraud

Exploitation of UPI collect requests, fake refund gateways, and deceptive QR codes designed to debit accounts under the guise of receiving payments.

Learn UPI safety →

Phishing Attacks

Forged lookalike portals cloning State Bank of India, HDFC, and Income Tax Department domains to harvest login credentials and PAN details.

Scan a suspicious link →

OTP & SIM Scams

Aggressive voice calls (vishing), deceptive 5G SIM upgrades, and remote desktop app installations (AnyDesk) used to intercept authentication codes.

View OTP protection tips →

Fake Websites

Typosquatted URLs using deceptive extensions (.xyz, .online) that abuse the illusion of HTTPS padlocks to steal credit card information.

Audit domain legitimacy →

Identity Theft

Misuse of Aadhaar and PAN documents through predatory unverified loan applications to extract contact lists and extort citizens.

Explore Aadhaar masking →

Digital Arrest Threats

Extortionists posing as CBI or Mumbai Police on video calls. Remember: Indian law does not recognize "Digital Arrest".

Get emergency 1930 help →

                  Critical Protocol

                  Evidence Preservation Checklist

                  To increase the probability of recovering funds or convicting criminals, follow these steps before reporting:

                  📸 1. Capture Full Screenshots

                  Take uncropped screenshots of all WhatsApp messages, SMS alerts, fake payment gateway screens, and phone call logs with timestamps visible.

                  🏦 2. Note Transaction UTR / Ref IDs

                  Record the 12-digit UPI Reference / UTR Number, beneficiary Virtual Payment Address (VPA), sender account number, and debit timestamp.

                  🔒 3. Block Compromised Cards & Accounts

                  Immediately call your bank’s official 24x7 emergency helpline to freeze compromised debit cards, UPI IDs, and NetBanking access.
